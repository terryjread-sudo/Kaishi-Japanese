Kaishi Quest v11.16.5 patch

Drop these files over the matching files in the live repo.

Changes:
- Japan Ready cheat-sheet speech no longer relies on a delayed setTimeout before the first speech call. It speaks synchronously from the user gesture, selects a Japanese voice when available, and can extract Japanese from the actual cheat-sheet card/button.
- Journey timeline is labelled Your Journey.
- Journey shows a window of three steps behind the current point and four ahead.
- Required side quests are normal route items: when triggered they are inserted into the timeline, followed by the retry of the same lesson.
- Japan Ready remains a separate path.
